# Age of War 2P

<img width="384" height="240" alt="PlayVideo" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/16fad285-fa21-4ff4-986f-22975951a1e6" />


This is a PVP adaptation of the classic Flash game, 'Age of War.'

In the original PVE version, the gameplay often revolved around building powerful turrets and using units to hold the line; however, in this PVP version, the damage and range of the turrets have been reduced to prevent such static play.

We hope this version offers a more engaging experience—encouraging you to produce a wide variety of units rather than simply waiting for your opponent to approach—while bringing back fond memories of the original game.

If you'd like to see gameplay footage, please follow the link.
[Youtube Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjE8zamyY24)

## How to Play

1. Produce units to destroy the enemy base.

2. Use special skills and turrets to defend your base from enemy incursions.

3. Eliminate enemy units to gain experience points and evolve to the next age.

## How to Download

Download the tagged file and run 'AgeOfWar2P.exe'.
